L26@P0: Nozzle Installation
L27@P0: Shrub
L28@P0: 1. If loose screen, place the filter screen, closed end first, into the shrub base. If integrated
L29@P0: screen, drop the entire nozzle assembly into the riser.
L30@P0: 2. Screw nozzle onto the shrub base and hand-tighten.
L31@P0: Pop-Up
L32@P0: 1. With your little finger, reach down into the riser (threads) and pull up.
L33@P0: 2. Grasp the riser above the cap firmly with your thumb and forefinger. Avoid using pliers
L34@P0: which may damage the riser.
L35@P0: 3. If loose screen, place the filter screen, closed end first, into the riser. If integrated screen,
L36@P0: drop the entire nozzle assembly into the riser.
L37@P0: 4. Screw the nozzle into the riser and hand-tighten.
L38@P0: 5. Release the riser slowly to the fully retracted position.
L39@P0: 6. If replacing the complete riser assembly (stem, spring and cap), align the keyways in the
L40@P0: riser with the body and screw on the cap. Do not over tighten the cap on the body as removal will be necessary to properly align spray patterns and flush the system.
L41@P0-1: 570S Shrub HeadSprinkler Installation
L42@P1: Caution: Use only PTFE tape to make threaded connections from riser or barbed ell to sprinkler body. Deterioration of plastic
L43@P1: may occur if other sealing compounds are used.
L44@P1: Note: It is recommended that shrub and pop-up sprinklers be installed on polyethylene risers or barbed ell and thick walled poly
L45@P1: pipe. The 12" high pop should be installed on a barbed ell and thick walled poly pipe to reduce trenching depth.
L46@P1: Shrub Sprinkler
L47@P1: 1. Screw assembled shrub sprinkler securely onto PTFE taped riser and position the spray pattern indicator on nozzle with the intended coverage area for part circle models.
L48@P1: Pop-Up Sprinkler
L49@P1: 1. Grasp the sprinkler by the body (not cap) and screw securely onto
L50@P1: the PTFE taped riser or barbed ell. Do not over tighten, hand-tight is
L51@P1: sufficient.
L52@P1: Note: The 570 Series pop-up sprinkler should be installed so that
L53@P1: the top of the cap is level with the final finished grade (soil).
L54@P1: 2. After all pop-up sprinkler heads have been installed, remove the cap
L55@P1: and riser from each body. For shrubs, remove nozzle and filter screen.
L56@P1: 3. Flush the system with water until all dirt and debris has been removed.
L57@P1: 4. Reinstall all shrub nozzles, filter screens, riser and cap assemblies,
L58@P1: positioning riser keyway of part circle models to properly align spray
L59@P1: pattern with intended coverage area. For adjustable arc nozzles, turn
L60@P1: the arc adjustment to the intended coverage area.
L61@P1: 5. Backfill the soil around the heads carefully, avoiding soil contact with
L62@P1: the top of the cap.
L63@P1: 6. Turn on the water source to the system.
L64@P1: 7. Adjust the spray radius as necessary. This is accomplished by turning
L65@P1: the slotted screw located on the top center of all nozzles.
L66@P1: A. To decrease radius (3' minimum), turn the adjustment screw clockwise. Precipitation rate and GPM will be reduced as well, depending upon working pressure of the system.
L67@P1: B. To increase the radius (15' maximum), turn the adjustment screw
L68@P1: counterclockwise.
