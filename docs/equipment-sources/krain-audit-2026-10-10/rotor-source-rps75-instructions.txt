L1@P0:  1. REMOVING THE NOZZLE RETENTION SCREW
L2@P0: Use the hex (B) end of the Key to remove the nozzle retention screw by turning
L3@P0: counter-clockwise to remove or clockwise to re-install.
L4@P0:  2. PULL UP THE RISER
L5@P0: Insert the (A) end of the Key in the keyhole (L) on the top of the nozzle turret (I)
L6@P0: and turn the key 1/4 turn to insure that the key does not slip out of the keyhole
L7@P0: when you pull it up. Firmly pull up the entire spring-loaded riser to access the
L8@P0: nozzle socket (G). Hold riser assembly with one hand.
L9@P0:  3. REMOVING THE NOZZLE
L10@P0: With nozzle retention screw removed, the nozzle may be removed by pulling
L11@P0: outward on the nozzle prongs (D) with a pair of needle-nose pliers.
L12@P0:  4. INSTALLING A NOZZLE
L13@P0: Press the desired nozzle (C) into the nozzle socket (G). Make sure the nozzle
L14@P0: number is visible and the nozzle “prongs” (D) are up. Then, re-install the nozzle
L15@P0: retention screw (F). NOTE: The nozzle retention screw is also a break-up screw,
L16@P0: used to adjust the distance of the spray.

L41@P0:  3. ADJUSTING THE LEFT (VARIABLE) SIDE OF THE ARC
L42@P0: INCREASING THE ARC
L43@P0: Insert the (A) end of the Key into the arc set adjustment slot (M).
L44@P0: While holding the nozzle turret (I) at the right start, turn the Key clockwise.
L45@P0: Each full 360° turn of the Key will increase the arc 90°. Adjust to any arc
L46@P0: between 40° and 360°. The Key will stop turning, or there will be ratcheting
L47@P0: noise, when the maximum arc of 360° has been reached.
L48@P0: DECREASING THE ARC
L49@P0: Insert the (A) end of the Key into the arc set adjustment slot (M). While
L50@P0: holding the nozzle turret (I) at the right start, turn the Key counter-clockwise.
L51@P0: Each full 360° turn of the Key will decrease the arc 90°. Adjust to any arc
L52@P0: between 40° and 360°. The Key will stop turning, or there will be a ratcheting
L53@P0: noise, when the minimum arc of 40° has been reached.

L55@P0:  1. INSTALL AND BURY
L56@P0: Do not use pipe dope. Thread the sprinkler on the pipe. Bury the sprinkler
L57@P0: NOTE: Gear driven sprinklers and pop-up
L58@P0: sprays should not be installed on the same watering zone.
L59@P0:  2. INSPECTING THE FILTER
L60@P0: Unscrew the top (H) and lift the complete sprinkler assembly (J) out of
L61@P0: the can (K)
L62@P0: and can be easily pulled out, cleaned and re-installed.
L63@P0:  3. WINTERIZATION TIPS
L64@P0: When using an air compressor to remove water
L65@P0: from the system please note the following:
L66@P0: a. Do not exceed 30 PSI.
L67@P0: b. Always introduce air into the system gradually to avoid
L68@P0: air pressure surges. Sudden release of compressed air
L69@P0: into the sprinkler can cause damage.
L70@P0: c. Each zone should run no longer than 1 minute on air.
L71@P0: Sprinklers turn 10 to 12 time faster on air than on water.
L72@P0: Over spinning rotors on air can cause damage to the
L73@P0: internal components.
