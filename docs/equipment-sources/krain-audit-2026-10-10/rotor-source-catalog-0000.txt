L66@P2: It’s not rocket science. Or is it?
L67@P2: Our story begins with a rocket scientist. A real rocket scientist. With a background in chemical engineering
L68@P2: and a stint in the Army Artillery Corps Guided Missile School, Carl Kah Jr. helped develop early reusable rocket
L69@P2: technology at Pratt & Whitney. That’s NASA rocket technology (still in use today mind you).
L70@P2: With a natural born instinct for invention, Carl turned his attention to his own landscape irrigation system and
L71@P2: thought, I can make it better. Call it: When science meets grass roots greatness. From the lathe in his garage,
L72@P2: he designed and machined a valve that cycled zone-to-zone and eliminated the need for multiple valves. That
L73@P2: creative inventiveness, or inventive creativity, led to patent number one in 1966.
L74@P2: With acres of ideas for greening up any size space, in 1970 Carl invented and patented a device he called
L75@P2: Modulated Pressure Control. It allowed for the control of the entire irrigation system of a golf course without wires
L76@P2: or tubes. The sale of that patent was the green light to incorporating K-Rain® Manufacturing in 1972.
L77@P2: Cultivating growth
L78@P2: Helping to grow the business from the ground up was son, Chip, who joined the company in 1986. Committed
L79@P2: to engineering products that are reliable and eco-conscious, Chip led the development and growth of indexing
L80@P2: valves for the wastewater disposal industry.
L81@P2: In 1991, Carl and Chip, always innovating, introduced the first of many patented gear drive rotors. Four years later,
L82@P2: K-Rain® augmented the professional line by expanding its products into the retail market - taking the business to
L83@P2: the next level.
L84@P2: Joining the burgeoning business that same year were Carl’s two daughters, Gretchen and Deborah. Gretchen
L85@P2: would lead growth in the west coast sales division. Deborah, an attorney, would be managing intellectual
L86@P2: property and human resources. And in 2016, Chip’s two sons, Christopher and Trevor, joined K-Rain® making it a
L87@P2: 3rd generation company.
L88@P2: Green and Growing the Industry
L89@P2: Look across the industry and you’ll find a little bit of K-Rain® innovation sprinkled into the competitive set. K-Rain®
L90@P2: holds over 100 patents specific to irrigation including the three-spring reversing mechanism still used today in
L91@P2: most gear-driven sprinklers.
L92@P2: Engineering the hardest-working irrigation products in the world is our heritage and our mission. Our
L93@P2: commitment to quality is reflected in an ISO 9001 quality certification - the standard for manufacturing and
L94@P2: process control.
L95@P2: We’re also environmentally conscious about the materials we select and the products we produce. Sustainability
L96@P2: is in our DNA. That’s why we’re an industry leader with a full range of products for reclaimed/recycled water.
