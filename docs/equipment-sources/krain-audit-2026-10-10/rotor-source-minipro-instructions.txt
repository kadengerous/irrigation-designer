L112@P1: 3. WINTERIZATION TIPS
L113@P1: When using an air compressor to remove water from the
L114@P1: system please note the following:
L115@P1: a. Do not exceed 30 PSI.
L116@P1: b. Always introduce air into the system gradually to avoid
L117@P1: air pressure surges. Sudden release of compressed
L118@P1: air into the sprinkler can cause damage.
L119@P1: c. Each zone should run no longer than 1 minute on air.
L120@P1: Sprinklers turn 10 to 12 time faster on air than on
L121@P1: water. Over spinning rotors on air can cause damage
L122@P1: to the internal components.
