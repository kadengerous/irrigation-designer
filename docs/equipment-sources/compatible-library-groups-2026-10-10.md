# Library compatibility groups — 2026-10-10

The library's top selection is the automatic-selection scope. Compact groups show all their models together. Hunter MP Standard has MP Rotator and MP Strip buttons; Pro Fixed has six buttons by model size with the catalog identification color; Bubblers & Streams has MSBN, PCN, PCB, AFB and CST buttons. The extensive Toro Precision Spray / PSN+ list retains its 12 browsing subdivisions. These subdivisions do not restrict automatic selection. A selector with fewer than two options is hidden. Each ordinary eligible emitter has one destination; Rain Bird 5004-PC and 5004+PC have two disjoint nozzle-rack views, not duplicate hydraulic configurations.

Compatibility and strip-layout advice lives in the right information panel, below the model or automatic-selection details. On phones it follows the existing contextual information surface: the selected card's details while browsing, and the floating inspector while placing. It no longer occupies the top of either library, and is not attached to the zone list or an unrelated placed head.

Manual-only browsing, including MP Strip, conventional strips and bubblers, omits the automatic-selection card and its unavailable-feature explanation. Entering such a subgroup ends an active automatic-placement draft. Card selection and manual placement remain available.

After the radius is fixed, the construction target stays on the outer sector endpoint, and an ordinary click uses that projected endpoint. The centre remains fixed. Hold the right mouse button (or macOS Ctrl+left) to pan the field with the hand cursor; releasing does not place, cancel, or edit the radius. The construction draft is preserved throughout panning. Escape cancels. The inspector radius input, middle-button/Space panning and touch/pinch gestures remain available.

The centre uses the normal snapping settings. Subsequent radius and sector construction references visible figure corners and contours only, with no grid or existing-head alignment. A second click with no radius candidate retains the dashed-radius phase; eligibility is recalculated at the release point against the current group and zone. Final placement retains its own validation.

Fixing the radius starts a separate sector step and cannot place a full circle. The sweep follows the pointer in either direction from that initial radius, including inward from opposite figure corners. Clockwise and counterclockwise construction both become the same canonical positive-angle geometry for preview, selection, placement and simulation. Contour snapping adjusts the displayed endpoint without adding another angular movement. A repeated click at an unswept starting edge does not request 360 degrees; the explicit 360-degree control or a complete traced revolution does.

Equipment group and subgroup buttons omit hover tooltips that merely repeat their visible names. Accessible button labels and informative equipment-status tooltips remain available on the shared desktop and phone surfaces.

The automatic-selection pressure input is explicitly the available pressure at the sprinkler. Drawing never replaces this user-entered limit with a selected model's pressure. The result shows the selected operating pressure separately, and identifies a pressure-regulating body when it reduces that pressure. Radius, discharge and compatibility continue to use the selected operating pressure; this is not a pipe-network pressure calculation. [Hunter's comparison](https://www.hunterirrigation.com/en-metric/node/28351) specifies PRS30 at 2.1 bar and PRS40 at 2.8 bar; [Rain Bird's 1800 data](https://www.rainbird.com/products/1800-series-spray-sprinkler-bodies) likewise distinguishes 2.1- and 3.1-bar regulated outlets. A nominal 2.1-bar body mapped to a published 2.07-bar chart does not, by itself, trigger the regulator-reduction note.

## Hydraulic checks and limits

- A manufacturer-declared matched class is used at recommended operating pressure. The documented Rain Bird PRS30 chart equivalents are normalized for that class. At other pressures, compare actual application rates; broad pressure bands must not mask a 31–54% mismatch between R-VAN and 5000 MPR settings.
- Other settings require the same computed/published square-scheme rate (retaining two decimal places), technology and operating pressure. This is a conservative program rule, not an invented manufacturer tolerance. It may keep otherwise engineer-approved configurations in separate zones.
- Automatic selection checks relevant existing heads in the active zone, and final placement runs the same selector again. Zone capacity and figure membership continue to be checked during assignment. Inner series never expand or shrink that candidate scope.
- Known-flow candidates at recommended pressure are preferred. Documented reduced-radius placement with unpublished discharge retains the existing review flag and cannot certify compatibility with an occupied zone.
- Strips, SQ, streams and bubblers retain manual placement: their shape/local dose cannot be represented by this radius-and-sector construction tool. A strip-layout caution appears even inside a matched family group.
- The radius phase searches familiar sectors first, then peer angles and all integer sectors when a zone constraint needs it. This prevents a false red preview for e.g. 137-degree VAN, or another rotor nozzle matching at a different sweep.
