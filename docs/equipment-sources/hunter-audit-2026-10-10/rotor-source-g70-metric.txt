L150: cite23†All Product Videos L151: ## Features
L152: Easy to adjust and maintain, Hunter's G-70 golf and large turf rotor is also the ideal choice for the customer on a budget. The B Series rotors provide all the standard features you want for mid-distance coverage including a time-tested gear drive, convenient through-the-top servicing, and a sturdy, impact-resistant flanged body. Plus Hunter's PressurePort™ nozzling system reduces velocity and pressure to the secondary nozzles while increasing droplet size.
L153: The result is they deliver uniform distribution every time.
L154: #### Key Benefits
L155: 
L156: Full circle water lubricated gear-drive
L157: 
L158: Closed-case design helps prevent sticking riser and leaking seals
L159: 
L160: Flexible performance with 6 nozzles to choose from
L161:
