L251: With 34 nozzle options, a 17’ to 46’ radius, and 6 body choices, the I-20 is an indispensable rotor. Whether you’ve got a small area, sandy soil, or a mix of shrubs and grass, the I-20 is ready to water with efficiency and reliability. Recent improvements include automatic arc return (non-reversing 360, from 50 to 360 degrees) and a non-strippable, vandal-proof drive mechanism that prevents gear stripping and saves repair costs.
L252: Adding FloStop^{®} control, easy, through-the-top arc adjustment, and a standard drain-check valve all equate to a rotor that stands second-to-none in its class.
