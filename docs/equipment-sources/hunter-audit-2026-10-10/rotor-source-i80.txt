L209: cite55†All Product Videos L210: ## Features
L211: 
L212: The I-80 Rotor is the most technologically advanced commercial rotor available. Engineered for sports turf and large park applications, the I-80 provides an ultra-robust, dirt-tolerant gear drive that offers the highest torque output of any rotor in the commercial arena.
L213: The I-80 features a wide range of highly efficient, dual trajectory, wind-fighting nozzles. Exclusive to the commercial rotor market, the I-80 provides Total-Top-Serviceability (TTS) via its integrated, surface-mounted snap ring. This no-dig solution makes maintenance quick and easy.
L214: #### Key Benefits
L215: 
L216: Exclusive Total-Top-Service (TTS) design provides convenient no-dig servicing
L217: 
L218: Exclusive PressurePort™ Technology creates exceptional nozzle uniformity
L219: 
L220: Forward-facing triple nozzles (I-80) or opposing triple nozzles (I-80-ON) create uniform nozzle profiles in part-and full-circle applications
L221: 
L222: QuickCheck™ Arc Mechanism (I-80) for fast arc adjustment and review of the arc setting
L223: 
L224: QuickSet-360 Arc Mechanism (I-80) converts adjustable arc rotor to full-circle in an instant
L225: Part- and full-circle in one model provides flexible installation options and reduces inventory (I-80)
L226: 
L227: Ratcheting stainless steel riser allows setting of right-side fixed arc alignment to the landscape without rotor disassembly
L228: 
L229: Drain check valve prevents low-head drainage (up to 5' of elevation)
