L135: cite33†  Download Written Specifications (in English) L136: ## Features
L137: 
L138: Designed for small-to-midsize synthetic turf field layouts, the STG-900 Rotor offers a radius range of up to 36.6 m. This versatile option can be installed in a vault system within the synthetic field.
L139: #### Key Benefits
L140: 
L141: Flexible arc adjustment from 40° to 360° offers narrow-to-wide coverage
L142: 
L143: QuickCheck™ Arc Mechanism lets you verify the arc setting without waiting for the sprinkler to finish its rotation
L144: 
L145: Through-the-top arc adjustment allows you to easily set the arc at the sprinkler head
L146: 
L147: Water-lubricated gear drive delivers reliable operation without clogging
L148: 
L149: User-installed rubber logo cap increases athlete safety on the field
L150: 
L151: Nozzle trajectory: 22.5°
L152: 
L153: #### Advanced Features
L154: ### STG-900
L155: 
L156: The STG-900 rotor comes standard with through-the-top access to the riser assembly for easy maintenance. This versatile option can also be installed in a vault system within the synthetic field. For added safety, order the user-installed rubber cover kit featuring a cushioned, non-slip surface over the exposed surface of the rotor.
