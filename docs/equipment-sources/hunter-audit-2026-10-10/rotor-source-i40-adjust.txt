L37: # Commercial Rotors - I-40 Rotor Adjustment Instructions
L38: 
L39: The I-40 rotor has an optional opposing nozzle, indicated by the model number ending in -ON. The adjustment instructions for the opposing nozzle model are listed separately from the non-opposing nozzle model.
L40: 
L41: I-40 Rotor Adjustment Sockets
L42: 
L43: cite10†Image L44: 
L45:   1. Arc adjustment socket
L46:   2. Nozzle-retainer / range-adjustment screw
L47:   3. Lift up socket
L48:   4. Indicates VStat™ self-adjusting stator
L49:   5. Specific model
L50: Arc Adjustment (Non-Opposing Nozzle Model)
L51: 
L52: cite11†Image L53: 
L54: All I-40 group adjustable heads are preset to approximately 180°. The I-40 can be adjusted from 50-360 degrees. Sprinklers may be adjusted with water on or off. It is recommended that initial adjustment be made before installation.
L55:   1. Using the palm of your hand, rotate the nozzle turret counter-clockwise to the left stop to complete any interrupted rotation cycle (Fig. 1).
L56:   2. Rotate the nozzle turret clockwise to the right stop. This is the fixed side of the arc, and it must be held in this position for all arc adjustments.
L57: 
L58: 
L59: 
L60: cite12†Image L61: 
L62: To Increase Arc
L63:   1. Insert the key end of the Hunter wrench into the adjustment socket.
L64:   2. While holding the nozzle turret at the right stop, turn the wrench clockwise. Each 360° turn of the wrench increases the arc 45°.
L65:   3. Adjust to any arc between 50° and 360°.
L66: 
L67:   4. Wrench will stop turning, or there will be a ratcheting noise, when the maximum arc (360°) is reached.
L68: 
L69:   5. When set to 360°, the sprinkler will rotate continually counterclockwise.
L70: 
L71: cite13†Image L72: 
L73: To Decrease Arc
L74:   1. Insert the key end of the Hunter wrench into the adjustment socket.
L75:   2. While holding the nozzle turret at the right stop, turn the wrench counterclockwise. Each 360° turn of the wrench decreases the arc 45°.
L76:   3. Adjust to any arc between 50° and 360°.
L77:   4. The wrench will stop turning, or there will be a ratcheting noise, when the minimum arc (40°) is reached.
L78: 
L79: Arc Adjustment (Opposing Nozzle Models)
L80: 
L81: All I-40-ON models are 360° ONLY. There is no arc adjustment. For less than 360° use I-40-ADS.
L82: Radius Adjustment
L83: 
L84: Insert the hex end of the Hunter wrench into the nozzle-retainer / range-adjustment screw. Turn the screw clockwise into the stream of water to decrease the radius, or counterclockwise to increase the radius.
L85: 
L86: Precipitation Rate Adjustment
L87: 
L88: Where excessively wet or dry areas are a problem, the precipitation rate may be adjusted. Simply replace the existing nozzle with a larger one to increase or a smaller one to decrease the rate of precipitation.
