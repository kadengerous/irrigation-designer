L50: Arc Adjustment
L51: 
L52: cite21†Image L53: All I-25 Ultra adjustable arc sprinklers are preset at approximately 180°. The I-25 can be adjusted from 50-360 degrees. Sprinklers may be adjusted with water on or off. It is recommended that initial adjustment be made before installation.
L54:   1. Using the palm of your hand, rotate the nozzle turret counterclockwise to left stop to complete any interrupted rotation cycle.
L55:   2. Rotate the nozzle turret clockwise to right stop. This is the fixed side of the arc. The nozzle turret must be held in this position for all arc adjustments.
L56: 
L57: cite22†Image L58: 
L59: To Increase Arc
L60: 
L61:   1. Insert the key end of the Hunter wrench into the adjustment socket.
L62:   2. While holding the nozzle turret at the right stop, turn the wrench clockwise. Each 360° turn of the wrench increases the arc 45°.
L63: 
L64:   3. Adjust to any arc between 50° and 360°.
L65: 
L66:   4. Wrench will stop turning, or there will be a ratcheting noise, when the maximum arc (360°) is reached.
L67: 
L68:   5. When set to 360°, the sprinkler will rotate continually counterclockwise.
L69: 
L70: cite23†Image L71: 
L72: To Decrease Arc
L73: 
L74:   1. Insert the key end of the Hunter wrench into the adjustment socket.
L75:   2. While holding the nozzle turret at the right stop, turn the wrench counterclockwise. Each 360° turn of the wrench decreases the arc 45°.
L76: 
L77:   3. Adjust to any arc between 50° and 360°.
L78: 
L79:   4. Wrench will stop turning, or there will be a ratcheting noise, when the minimum arc (50°) is reached.
L80: 
L81: Radius Adjustment
L82: Insert the hex end of the Hunter wrench into the nozzle-retainer/range-adjustment screw. Turn the screw clockwise into the stream of water to decrease the radius, or counterclockwise to increase the radius.
L83: 
L84: Precipitation Rate Adjustment
L85: 
L86: Where excessively wet or dry areas are a problem, the precipitation rate may be adjusted. Simply replace the existing nozzle with a larger one to increase or a smaller one to decrease the rate of precipitation.
