# PS Ultra

The PS Ultra is a slim-line spray sprinkler with the option of factory-installed nozzles for faster installation.

Radius 2.5 cm to 5.2 m (with Pro High-Efficiency Nozzles)

Radius Fixed 1.5 m x 9.1 m side strip

Models 5 cm, 10 cm, 15 cm

Inlet ½"

Product Line

Spray Bodies

  * Simple: Installs faster with optional factory-installed Pro High-Efficiency Nozzles
  * Efficient: Waters more efficiently with a high-uniformity spray pattern for consistent coverage
  * Compact: Built tough for tight spaces with a durable slim-line spray body
  * Versatile: Works with all nozzles for easy retrofitting

## Specifications

Operating Specifications

  * Operational pressure range: 1.4 to 4.8 bar; 140 to 480 kPa
  * Warranty period: 2 years
  * Male-threaded riser
  * Double filtration on 10 cm and 15 cm models

Factory-Installed Options

  * Flush plug (large filter screen not included)
  * Nozzles: 8A-HE, 10A-H, 12A-HE, 15A-HE, 17A-HE, 5SS (1.5 m x 9.1 m side strip)
  * Large inlet filter screen included in 10 cm and 15 cm preinstalled nozzle models

User-Installed Options 

  * Check valve installs in filter screen for 10 cm and 15 cm models (up to 2 m of elevation): P/N 462237SP
  * Large inlet filter screen: P/N 162900SP
  * Shutoff nozzle: P/N 916400SP

## Features

Slim-line PS Ultra Sprinkler Bodies with Pro High-Efficiency Nozzles give contractors a faster, more efficient way to install traditional spray systems. Designed for tight spaces, PS Ultra combines a compact body with durable internal components for long-term reliability.

The factory-installed Pro High-Efficiency Nozzle improves water efficiency with a high-uniformity spray pattern and matched precipitation across the 2.5 m to 5.2 m range. Hunter must approve the design prior to final production.
