L142: cite34†All Product Videos L143: ## Features
L144: Easy to adjust and maintain, Hunter's G-70 golf and large turf rotor is also the ideal choice for the customer on a budget. The B Series rotors provide all the standard features you want for mid-distance coverage including a time-tested gear drive, convenient through-the-top servicing, and a sturdy, impact-resistant flanged body. Plus Hunter's PressurePort™ nozzle system reduces velocity and pressure to the secondary nozzles while increasing droplet size.
L145: The result is they deliver uniform distribution every time.
L146: #### Key Benefits
L147: 
L148: G-70-B is a dedicated, true full-circle model
