L156: cite41†All Product Videos L157: ## Features
L158: Easy to adjust and maintain, Hunter's G-80-B golf and large turf rotor is also the ideal choice for the customer on a budget. The B Series rotors provide all the standard features you want for mid-distance coverage including a time-tested gear drive, convenient through-the-top servicing, and a sturdy, impact-resistant flanged body. Plus Hunter's PressurePort™ nozzle system reduces velocity and pressure to the secondary nozzles while increasing droplet size.
L159: The result is they deliver uniform distribution every time.
L160: #### Key Benefits
L161: 
L162: Dedicated, true full-circle model distinguished by a black collar
L163: 
L164: Exclusive PressurePort™ Nozzle Technology optimizes incoming pressure at each nozzle to increase consistency and maximize distribution uniformity
L165: 
L166: High-torque gear drive is the strongest in the industry to mitigate the challenges of debris infiltration
L167: 
L168: #### Advanced Features
L169: ### B Series Rotors are Yardage Marker Capable
L170: 
L171: All B Series rotors have a recessed area on the flange to accommodate installation of custom yardage markers.
