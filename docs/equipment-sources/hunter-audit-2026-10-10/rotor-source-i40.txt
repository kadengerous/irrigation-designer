L236: cite54†All Product Videos L237: ## Features
L238: The I-40 is a tough commercial rotor that delivers water with accuracy and efficiency at distances up to 70 feet. Featuring diverse, three-port nozzle options, a non-strippable drive, and a thick rubber cover built to prevent injury, it's no wonder this rotor is found in more stadiums than any other in the world. With the strength of stainless steel, the I-40 is built to last in the harshest sports fields, public parks, and commercial environments.
L239: If efficiency, effective irrigation, and beautiful results are the goal, the I-40 is the irrigation professional's best solution.
L240: #### Key Benefits
L241: 
L242: Patented automatic arc return feature returns the turret back to the original arc pattern if vandalized; adjustable arc from 50° to 360°
L243: 
L244: Non-strippable drive mechanism is protected from damage if turned in the opposite direction of travel
L245: 
L246: Part- and full-circle in one model for flexibility across landscapes and reduced inventory
L247: 
L248: Color-coded nozzles make identification easy
L249: 
L250: Available opposing nozzle model for even watering in full-circle applications (I-40-ON model)
L251: Drain Check Valve prevents low-head drainage (up to 15' of elevation)
L252: #### Advanced Features
L253: 
L254: ### Opposing Nozzle 360˚ Model
L255: 
L256: The opposing nozzle design offers excellent water distribution. With primary and secondary nozzles on opposing sides of the turret, streams arc in opposite directions as the sprinkler rotates for outstanding mid-range and close-in watering.
