L132@P0: All I-80 adjustable arc rotors are preset to approximately 180°.
L133@P0: The rotors can be adjusted with water on or off. It is recommended
L134@P0: that initial arc adjustments be made before installation. Use your
L135@P0: hand to rotate the nozzle turret counterclockwise to the left stop to
L136@P0: complete any interrupted rotation cycle. Rotate the nozzle turret
L137@P0: clockwise to the right stop. This is the fixed side of the arc. The
L138@P0: nozzle turret must be held in this position for all arc adjustments.
L139@P0: The ratchet feature allows the right fixed stop to be moved when the
L140@P0: water is off. To adjust the right fixed arc, pull the riser up, grab the
L141@P0: riser below the adjustment ring, and rotate the right fixed arc stop to
L142@P0: the desired landscape alignment.
