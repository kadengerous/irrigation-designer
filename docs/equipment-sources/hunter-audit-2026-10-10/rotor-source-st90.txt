L130: cite31† Download Product Spec Sheet L131: ## Features
L132: 
L133: The perfect solution for small-to-midsize synthetic turf field layouts. Designed for installation adjacent to the synthetic field, the ST-90 rotor provides a cost-competitive solution with a radius range up to 120'.
L134: #### Key Benefits
L135: 
L136: Flexible arc adjustment from 40° to 360° offers narrow-to-wide coverage
L137: 
L138: QuickCheck™ Arc Mechanism lets you verify the arc setting without waiting for the sprinkler to finish its rotation
L139: 
L140: Through-the-top arc adjustment allows you to easily set the arc at the sprinkler head
L141: 
L142: Water-lubricated gear drive delivers reliable operation without clogging
L143: 
L144: Factory-installed rubber logo cap increases athlete safety on the field
L145: 
L146: Nozzle trajectory: 22.5°
L147: 
L148: #### Advanced Features
L149: ### ST-90
L150: 
L151: cite32†Image†www.hunterindustries.com L152: 
L153: The ST-90 rotor features jar-top access to the riser assembly for easy maintenance and a smaller exposed surface area that minimizes sprinkler contact for greater player safety.
