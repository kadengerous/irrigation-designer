L162: cite33†All Product Videos L163: ## Features
L164: Easy to adjust and maintain, Hunter's G-75 golf and large turf rotor is also the ideal choice for the customer on a budget. The B Series rotors provide all the standard features you want for mid-distance coverage including a time-tested gear drive, convenient through-the-top servicing, and a sturdy, impact-resistant flanged body. Plus Hunter's PressurePort™ nozzling system reduces velocity and pressure to the secondary nozzles while increasing droplet size.
L165: The result is they deliver uniform distribution every time.
L166: #### Key Benefits
L167: 
L168: Adjustable arc water lubricated gear-drive
L169: 
L170: 50 to 360 degree adjustable arc range
L171: 
L172: Arc adjustable anytime; un-installed, installed or while in operation
L173: 
L174: QuickSet-360 adjustable arc and non-reversing full-circle in one rotor
L175: 
L176: Quick check arc mechanism makes arc adjust fast and easy
L177: 
L178: Closed-case design helps prevent sticking riser and leaking seals
L179:
