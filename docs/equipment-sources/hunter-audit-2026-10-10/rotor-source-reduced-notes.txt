L75@P1: A Guide to the Charts
L76@P1: In a distribution profile (Figure 1a), the sprinkler is located on the left side.
L77@P1: Catchment cans are placed in a straight row in two foot increments. The sprinkler is
L78@P1: then allowed to operate as it normally would under field conditions for a predetermined amount of time. At the completion of the test, the water in the catch cans is
L79@P1: measured and the results are plotted on the chart. This is the actual amount of water
L80@P1: that falls in the catch cans (distribution uniformity, or DU).
L81@P1: The scheduling coefficient (SC) is shown on the cart in Figure 1b. The SC measures
L82@P1: the uniformity of the application of water by the sprinkler. It is a measure of how much
L83@P1: water falls into one catch can as it relates to the amount falling into the rest of the cans.
L84@P1: A perfect SC of 1.0 states that all catchments fill to the same level.

L125@P3: Summary
L126@P3: Hunter nozzle sets have been
L127@P3: designed for optimum performance
L128@P3: when installed “head to head.” With
L129@P3: the PGP sprinklers, the fine mist
L130@P3: from the secondary orifices supplies
L131@P3: more than enough water in the area
L132@P3: close to the heads, especially if it is
L133@P3: supplemented with some of the
L134@P3: range spray from the adjacent heads.
L135@P3: Additionally, the performance of
L136@P3: the nozzles tends to be better in the
L137@P3: middle of the recommended pressure range.
L138@P3: Use the nozzle closest to the radius of throw you need and do not
L139@P3: diffuse it unless reduced radius is
L140@P3: required to keep water away from
L141@P3: hardscape areas. If there is a coverage problem, do not assume that a
L142@P3: new or different nozzle will solve
L143@P3: the problem.
L144@P3: If the system is designed for headto-head coverage and the range
L145@P3: spray is not reaching the adjacent
L146@P3: sprinkler, the probable cause is not
L147@P3: the nozzle that was supplied.
L148@P3: Look for other sources of the
L149@P3: problem, such as pressure (too high
L150@P3: or too low), heads that are not
L151@P3: perpendicular to the turf, damaged
L152@P3: nozzles, leaks in the system, and
L153@P3: nozzles not installed properly.
L154@P3: Check these prior to changing or
L155@P3: adjusting the nozzles.
