L156: Easy to adjust and maintain, Hunter's G-75 golf and large turf rotor is also the ideal choice for the customer on a budget. The B Series rotors provide all the standard features you want for mid-distance coverage including a time-tested gear drive, convenient through-the-top servicing, and a sturdy, impact-resistant flanged body. Plus Hunter's PressurePort™ nozzle system reduces velocity and pressure to the secondary nozzles while increasing droplet size.
L157: The result is they deliver uniform distribution every time.
