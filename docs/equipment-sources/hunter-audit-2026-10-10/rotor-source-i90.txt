L196: cite43†All Product Videos L197: ## Features
L198: The I-90 is Hunter’s longest-distance rotor specifically designed for natural turf, parks, sports fields, and public areas. With a radius of up to 101', the I-90 boasts the longest throw capability of all natural turf rotors. Yet its reduced-size diameter and cushioned rubber cover make it the perfect choice for parks and wide-open lawn areas, as well as perimeter watering of athletic fields and horse arenas.
L199: The rotor is also easy to service, with a “jar-top” body cap that allows quick access to the filter screen and check valve with no extra pieces, parts, or tools required. The I-90 is offered in two models: a full-circle opposing-nozzle model and an adjustable arc model, each with color-coded nozzles that can be field-changed to match the specific needs of any large site.
L200: #### Key Benefits
L201: 
L202: Exclusive PressurePort™ Technology creates exceptional nozzle uniformity
L203: 
L204: Forward-facing triple nozzles (I-90) or opposing triple nozzles (I-90-ON) create uniform nozzle profiles in part- and full-circle applications
L205: 
L206: QuickCheck™ Arc Mechanism for fast arc adjustment and review of the arc setting
L207: 
L208: Part- and full-circle in one model provides flexible installation options and decreases inventory (I-90)
L209: 
L210: Drain Check Valve prevents low-head drainage (up to 7' of elevation)
L211: #### Advanced Features
L212: 
L213: ### Opposing Nozzle 360˚ Model
L214: 
L215: The opposing nozzle design offers excellent water distribution. With primary and secondary nozzles on opposing sides of the turret, streams arc in opposite directions as the sprinkler rotates for outstanding mid-range and close-in watering.
L216: 
L217: cite44†Image: The I-40 nozzle in use. L218: ### I-90 Dual Trajectory Color-Coded Nozzles
L219: 
L220: All I-90 Series rotors have color-coded nozzles for easy identification and, the nozzle numbers approximate the flow in gallons per minute. Choose from the eight standard 22.5 degree trajectory nozzles or the eight 15 degree nozzles for low-angle applications.

L230: 
L231: Purple logo caps indicate the use of non-potable irrigation. They are available as a factory installed-option or as a replacement part.

L235: ### I-90 DRAIN CHECK VALVE (up to 7' of Elevation)
L236: 
L237: As air in the system piping can cause long-term damage to rotors and plumbing, the drain check valve keeps lines from draining when the system is shut off. This saves water, reduces liability, and increases system life.
