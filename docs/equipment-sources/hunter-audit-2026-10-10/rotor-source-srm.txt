L200: cite36†All Product Videos L201: ## Features
L202: The SRM is Hunter’s shortest-range rotor, and was designed to offer a convenient and efficient alternative to spray their. It is capable of working in tandem with larger rotors to combine big and small areas in a single zone. Utilizing the SRM where appropriate results in fewer sprinklers performing more efficient work at a more economical price. The SRM features a rack of six water-efficient nozzles—all of which can be easily identified by its flow rates and installed and changed quickly and easily.
L203: This versatile rotor also includes a radius adjustment feature that fine-tunes the spray to keep water off driveways, walks, and other non-landscaped surfaces.
L204: #### Key Benefits
L205: 
L206: Adjustable arc from 40° to 360° to keep water in the appropriate areas
L207: 
L208: Standard factory-installed 2.0 nozzle speeds installation
L209: 
L210: Through-the-top arc adjustment for easy installation
L211: 
L212: QuickCheck™ Arc Mechanism for fast arc adjustment
