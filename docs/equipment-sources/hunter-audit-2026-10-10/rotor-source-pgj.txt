L222: cite48†All Product Videos L223: ## Features
L224: The PGJ was built to bring all the efficient benefits of rotary sprinklers to applications that typically call for a spray. Its many features include a rack of easily installable, water-efficient nozzles, a QuickCheck^{TM} Arc Mechanism, and a durable rubber cover. The PGJ is capable of working in tandem with larger rotors to combine big and small areas in a single zone, offering a convenience and efficiency that sprays do not.
L225: When used in the right application, the PGJ allows fewer heads to perform more efficiently at a more economical price.
L226: #### Key Benefits
L227: 
L228: Headed and slotted setscrew allows radius adjustment with a Hunter Wrench or flat-blade screwdriver
L229: 
L230: Adjustable arc from 40° to 360° to keep water in the appropriate areas
L231: 
L232: Standard factory-installed 2.0 nozzle speeds installation
L233: 
L234: QuickCheck™ Arc Mechanism for fast arc adjustment
L235:
