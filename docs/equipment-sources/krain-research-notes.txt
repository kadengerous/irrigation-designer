RADIUS RULES

No numeric universal reduction percentage and no reduced-radius discharge rows in current sheet/catalog. Actual table radii can exceed advertised range; catalog-ready rMax accommodates tabulated rows and advertisedRadiusRangeM preserves overview bounds.
Fully Adjustable:2026p42 says up to30% radius reduction with flow control, matched precipitation; no reduced-radius flow rows. Older brochure inconsistently mentions25% and30%; current page says30%.
KVF/KV/fixed:radius reduction screw allowed; no numeric percent or adjusted discharge table found. Do not apply a generic25% or square-law flow assumption as published data.
MiniPro/RPS50/RPS75/RPSSelect/ProPlus:FAQ permits up to25% radius reduction using retention screw, max3turns. Adjusted-radius flows are not tabulated. radiusAdjustment.flowPolicy remains unknown.
RPS75i/SuperPro:FAQ allows flow-control radius/flow reduction up to50%; current2026catalog publishes -30% endpoints only. Do not treat -30% rows as minimum radius or fabricate a -50% flow from linear scaling. RPS75i olderRev4 -50% rows are preserved separately with edition/pressure conflicts.
All rotor precipitation uses180-degree operation; manufacturer says divide by2for360. A nozzle's flow is not automatically scaled by arc.
